# Tile-aware quantization: results summary for presentation

Status date: 2026-11-06. Branch `tile-aware` (PR https://github.com/ucb-ee290c/atlas-torchao/pull/5). Every number below names its source. Items marked not run were not produced.

## 1. What changed

| | Before (`atlas_v1_awq`) | Tile-aware |
|---|---|---|
| Weight exponent | one per output channel | one per 32 output channels, constant over all of K |
| Activation exponent | one per token, run-time | one per 32-token block, or one static value per site |
| Applied by hardware | not natively: needs extra vector multiplies around the matrix multiply | once, at pop: weight and activation exponents fold into one scale |
| Exponent entries (TinyLlama) | 394,240 | 12,320 |

Why: the matrix unit multiplies unscaled E4M3 and accumulates in BF16 across K tiles; a power-of-two scale can only be applied when a tile is popped, packed or unpacked, so only exponents constant along K are hardware-native. (`docs/algos/tile-aware.md`)

## 2. Deliverables

- Flow: `w=panel`, `a=block`, `attn=block`; recipes `atlas_v1_awq_tile`, `atlas_v1_tile`, `atlas_v1_awq_tile_static`.
- Verifier for the tile contract (export and replay) and an independent bundle checker (`scripts/tile_flow/verify_bundle.py`, 25 checks).
- How-to from any model to the FX graph: `docs/algos/howto-model-to-fx-graph.md`; artifact page https://claude.ai/artifact/CXCiae1VpYf6UsqsoD7QJp (private).
- Verified native FP8 FX bundles under `/scratch2/lorenhung/atlas-fp8/exports-tile/`: TinyLlama (2 recipes), SmolVLA, Multi-Task DiT, Pi0 (including `gptq_panel_static_noawq_deadrtn`, the Pi0 candidate).
- Opt-in exact pack-range fold (`--pack-fold`, `docs/algos/pack-fold.md`) and per-phase graphs with a host loop manifest for DiT and Pi0 (`docs/algos/phase-graphs.md`).

### Metadata size (source: `tile_vs_nontile_comparison.json`)

## 4. Findings

1. **AWQ with panel exponents damages salient rows** (Pi0). Gemma layer-9 q-input relative L2: 0.1273 (tile export) versus 0.0252 (previous export). Swapping the one Gemma layer-0 `up_proj` tensor from a per-channel export gives 0.0230. About 53 of 16384 rows are damaged. Ruled out: missing norm fold, activations, calibration frames, shape bugs. (`docs/algos/tile-pi0-awq-diag.md`, commit 57298dd)
   - Mitigation, tile-native: GPTQ on the panel grid with AWQ off gives layer 9 at 0.0237 and layer 17 at 0.0207 (old per-channel export 0.0252 and 0.0223). Full bundle `exports-tile/pi0/gptq_panel_static_noawq/`: 420 `qlinear`, 63 attention pairs, checker 25 of 25, fresh-process replay passed, 124 of 483 static sites outside 122..128 (`docs/algos/pi0-gptq-panel-noawq.md`, commit a349620). Drift only; no end-to-end action quality.
   - Fix: producer-aware AWQ search (option `producer_aware=true`, default off, bit-identical when off; `docs/algos/awq-producer-aware.md`). Adds the producer's panel-grid quantization error to the AWQ loss. Weights-only proxy on Pi0 Gemma layer 0: damaged producer rows 4 (old search) to 0 (new), composite error 0.605 to 0.028. 10 new tests (9 pass, 1 skips without submodules). NOT validated end to end: no Pi0 export with the option and no layer-9 drift measurement; the proxy search does not reproduce the recorded alphas.
   - TinyLlama does not show the damage (weights-only scan, `docs/algos/tinyllama-ablation.md`): AWQ fold scale range at most 4.87 binades against 19 for Pi0, within-panel row-amax spread of folded `up_proj` at most 6.0x median and 9.9x at the 95th percentile (Pi0 rows sat about 12 binades below their panel maximum), composite error median 0.0449 tile versus 0.0450 per-channel. The producer-aware search showed no regression on TinyLlama layers 0 and 1 (MLP error -0.7% and +0.4%).
2. **SigLIP `mlp.fc2` weights: the apparent 97% flush is dead columns zeroed by GPTQ, not E4M3 flush** (Pi0, both the old per-channel and the new export; `docs/algos/siglip-fc2-flush.md`, commit a6cd07e). In layer 23, 4177 of 4304 input columns are entirely zero (0.9706 of the codes) because `src/algos/gptq_awq.py:107-109` zeroes any column whose calibration Hessian diagonal is zero, and the GELU output into those channels was exactly zero on the calibration frames. Round-to-nearest on the same exponents flushes 0.035% and gives weight error 0.0266, so there are no outlier weights and the `mse` rule, smaller `rows` and per-row exponents change nothing. On one real DROID frame the layer 23 output error is 0.0244 for the export (RTN 0.0254); on Gaussian inputs that excite every column it is 0.85 to 0.98 versus 0.0266. The alive channel set varies by frame (72 per image, 127 over the 8 calibration frames), so a new frame can hit a zeroed column. Actions not measured, one frame only. Fix implemented as an opt-in option `dead_columns=rtn` (default `zero`, bit-identical when off; `docs/algos/gptq-dead-columns.md`, commit 7f5d9a3, 13 new tests, 115 passed across the six affected test files): dead columns are cast to nearest on the same exponents and give no error feedback, live columns are bit-identical to `zero`. Validated on SigLIP layer 23 `fc2` only (one frame; the export's own 8-frame Hessian was not reproduced): weight error 0.0411 against 0.9894, Gaussian output error 0.0407 against 0.9891, and on a held-out image the output error is 0.0226 against 0.1156 for `zero`. Not validated: no Pi0 re-export, no action quality. Alternative: keep layers 17 to 25 `fc2` in BF16 (10.9% of SigLIP linear multiply-accumulates) with `mixed_precision:units=...,keep_as=bf16`. The same mechanism zeroes 24 of 32 `state_proj` columns (padding), which is harmless.
   - **Pi0 re-export with `dead_columns=rtn`** (GPTQ panel, AWQ off; `exports-tile/pi0/gptq_panel_static_noawq_deadrtn/`, `docs/algos/pi0-deadrtn.md`, same 8 calibration frames, checker 25 of 25): SigLIP zero columns 40,392 down to 305 (all in layer 1 `fc2`; plain RTN on the same grid gives the same 305), `fc2` layers 17 to 25 from about 3.5k to 4.2k each down to 0, Gemma 635 down to 0. Mean weight error against stock 0.0605 down to 0.0355; layers above 0.1: 15 down to 2; `state_proj` 0.839 down to 0.037. Held-out image: `rtn` never worse than `zero`, 1.7 to 5.1 times lower in layers 22 to 25. Gemma drift L9/L17 0.0244/0.0222 against 0.0237/0.0207 for the previous no-AWQ export (3 to 7% higher, not isolated; exports are not bit-reproducible, 2 of 483 activation exponents differ). Action error against stock (`delta_rel_l2`, held-out observations): 4 observations 0.0564 previous, 0.0594 new; 8 observations 0.0574 previous, 0.0539 new; not separable. Not measured: closed loop, run-to-run spread, FX output against stock.
3. **Compiler pack range 122..128.** Static activation sites outside the range: TinyLlama 114 of 176, SmolVLA 244 of 316, DiT 88 of 203, Pi0 127 of 483 (`docs/algos/pack-range-options.md`, `pack-plan-census.md`). A power-of-two fold into the producer is exact: 0 differing FP8 bytes over 116,654,080 elements at the 92 out-of-range TinyLlama Linear inputs. Clamping instead changes quantization (TinyLlama perplexity 12.8756 clamp all, 12.8609 Linear only, 12.9027 `p` only, 12.8980 unclamped). Sites where the fold costs no added instruction: 92 / 221 / 24 / 61. Attention `p` needs one multiply per head and 32-row block. Exporter now records a per-site plan (`pack_plan` in `policy.json`, informational; `--require-pack-range` to enforce). Not run on RTL or hardware.
   - **Fold implemented** (opt-in `--pack-fold`, `docs/algos/pack-fold.md`, merge 6896f68; default exports byte-identical to the base commit). It also rescales the consumer's weight exponent by -k so the pop constant `2^(sx+sw)` is unchanged. Results: TinyLlama 114 of 176 sites outside the range drops to 22 (all attention `p`), folded versus unfolded logits identical (16,384,000 values, 0 differing), checker 25 of 25. SmolVLA 244 of 316 drops to 63, eager and FX bit-identical, checker 25 of 25. DiT 88 to 64 and Pi0 127 to 66 are counts only, not captured. Correction to the census: SmolVLA folds 181, not 221 (32 P.V sites share one text-attention site with 24 `pv_matmul` nodes and are refused; 8 VLM `v_proj` sites are also read by a dynamic attention quantizer). `pack_plan` still reports 40 SmolVLA sites as free that the fold refuses. New trailing `pop_shift` argument on `atlas.pv_matmul`: the compiler importer must accept it.
4. **Reporting defects fixed** (commit 09ef1df): uncalibrated Linears under a static recipe are declared and not marked tile-native; dynamic admission reads `not_checked_dynamic`; declared-versus-unexpected contractions in the verifier; Pi0 manifest crash fixed.
5. **Like-for-like cycles, old per-row versus tile-aware, TinyLlama** (measured by the peer session on `npu_model` with `numerics=rtl`, not by us; `/scratch2/lorenhung/atlas-bringup/work/asis-layers/tile_cycles_tl/results.md`): 110 plain-projection launches (layers 0 and 10; q, o, gate, up at 32x2048x256; down at 32x5632x1024), 110 of 110 golden-exact, arena equals export. Unmodified program plus host correction: 459,785 cycles (32x2048x256) and 4,501,837 (down) on all four exports. Per-row on-device rescale variants cost +2.7% to +2.9% (+1.1% to +1.2% on down; `a1b` +2.76%, +1.13%). Tile-aware variant `c` (one 4-byte scalar per 32 output columns applied at pop) on tile_static, tile and packfold: 460,540 to 460,622 cycles (+0.16% to +0.18%) and 4,504,708 to 4,504,748 on down (about +0.06%). Packfold: all 20 projection launches accepted and exact; weight codes byte-identical to tile_static. Caveats: the harness reads only `export/model.safetensors`, never the FX graph, so the trailing `pop_shift` on `atlas.pv_matmul` is NOT exercised and no PV launch was run; packfold activations are the tile recordings times 2^delta (inferred), so no packfold accuracy against BF16; RTL cycles are `npu_model` minus one (inferred, no replay). Pi0 candidate measurement is in progress at the peer.
6. **Pi0 candidate on the hand-written backend** (peer session, not measured by us; `/scratch2/lorenhung/atlas-bringup/work/asis-layers/tile_pi0_deadrtn/results.md`): 75 launches (72 T1, 3 T2-c), arena equals export 75/75, golden exact 75/75, byte-flip detected 75/75, 8/8 RTL replays whole-arena exact (unpinned 0079c05). The export sha256 equals `bundle_manifest.json`; the harness did not use `fx/`. Site-input drift L9/L17 0.0244/0.0222, level with the earlier panel arm and `atlas_v1_awq`, far better than `tile_static` (0.1273/0.0852). **Large device errors against BF16 remain and are unchanged by the recipe:** Gemma `down` layers 1 to 11 are 12.5% to 37.1% off BF16 (`atlas_v1_awq` sweep 11.9% to 37.3%); SigLIP `fc2` layers 0 to 3 are 12.3% to 45.4% (`atlas_v1_awq` 12.6% to 45.3%). Exact FP8 math (fp64) on the same codes and activations is only 1.2% to 2.7% (Gemma `down`) and 0.7% to 1.7% (SigLIP `fc2`) off BF16, so the 12% to 45% is the gap between device output and exact math; the peer's inference (not isolated) is BF16 K-ordered accumulation on long K, not the weight recipe. Exception: Gemma `down` layer 7, 18.1% from the cast alone (83% of non-zero activations flush to zero in the FP8 cast). Caveat: 23.8% of weight code bytes differ from the earlier panel export and only 3 of 420 sites are identical, so `dead_columns=rtn` cannot be isolated from other differences. This is a hardware accumulation question, not something the software flow fixes.
7. **Earlier hardware findings from the peer harness** (not measured by us): variants `c` (one scalar per 32-column tile) +0.02% to +0.18% cycles, `cb` (one scalar per 32x32 output tile) +0.23% (`/scratch2/lorenhung/atlas-bringup/work/asis-layers/tile/results.md`).

## 5. Limits

- Per-phase graphs now exist (merge 4794e1e, `docs/algos/phase-graphs.md`): DiT `encode` and `step` (strict, 25 of 25 each), Pi0 `prefix` and `step` (non-strict, 25 of 25 each), with a `loop_manifest.json` (DiT 100 DDPM steps, Pi0 10 Euler steps; update rule, noise draws and clipping stay on the host). DiT: `step(encode(obs))` is byte-identical to the single-step bundle and the 100-step host loop equals eager `conditional_sample` byte for byte (max abs 0.0). Pi0: one step through the phases is byte-identical to the single-step graph on both cases, which also shows stacked cameras equal three SigLIP calls on the real weights. Pi0 10-step host loop against eager `sample_actions(num_steps=10)`: byte-identical on both cases (max abs 0.0, 0 of 320 elements differ, one process at 8 threads, one noise draw per case; commit 0913311). This is a consistency check of the phase split and host loop, not an accuracy check. Open discrepancy: the recorded fixtures of the existing single-step bundles do not reproduce bit-exactly on this host (DiT case 0 max abs 0.0234, Pi0 0.036 and 0.045); the existing `model.pt2` and eager both differ from the fixture, the result depends on CPU kernel dispatch, root cause not found. Provenance (host bwrcix-1, torch builds) matches, so a different host or torch is not supported (CPU model and thread count were not recorded). The Pi0 single-step output changes with thread count (1, 4, 8 threads each differ from each other and from the fixture by 0.04 to 0.06), which is consistent with a fixture recorded at another thread count or load but is not shown. The in-process comparison is the evidence. Pi0 `model.pt2` files are 4.1 to 4.3 GB because unused modules' lifted buffers are not pruned.
- Earlier statement for the single-step graphs: DiT and Pi0 graphs are partial: DiT is one noise-predictor call; Pi0 is a one-step sampler captured non-strict under torch 2.7.1 with three cameras stacked into one SigLIP call (bitwise equal to three calls on a tiny Pi0 and a real-width two-layer SigLIP; real checkpoint not run). No Pi0 or DiT boundary spec exists from the compilers team. (`docs/algos/dit-pi0-full-graph-plan.md`)
- DiT's own attention: the six `RoPEAttention` blocks are now attention sites (7 new tests pass; 77 passed and 16 skipped with the tile and native tests). Re-exported bundle `exports-tile/multitask-dit/atlas_v1_awq_tile_static_attn/`: 30 qk and 30 pv pairs, 209 of 209 sites tile-native, checker 25 of 25, strict capture and fresh-process replay passed (`docs/algos/dit-own-attention.md`). Static sites outside 122..128: 94 of 209 (the 6 new `p` sites at code 119 added 6). Attention matrices have 24 tokens, a partial tile; the compiler must confirm tail-tile handling. No DiT quality number, dropout not modelled.

- No hardware equivalence claim: exact float32 accumulation plus `sa`/`ipt` models on nine isolated TinyLlama layers; no RTL run by us.

Rerun on the branch at 154e08c: 889 passed, 35 skipped, 10 deselected, 0 failed, in 22 minutes 19 seconds. After merging pack-fold and phase graphs (4794e1e): see the line below. ## 6. Decisions needed (full list in `/scratch2/lorenhung/atlas-fp8/tile-aware-checkin.md`)

1. Compiler owner: widen the pack range, or accept exporter folds (recommended order: exact folds, compiler expansion as fallback, widening after RTL tests). Folds are opt-in today and need the importer to accept `pop_shift`; say if folded bundles should be the default.
2. Pi0: ship with AWQ off and `dead_columns=rtn` (taken as the candidate; zero-column fix is correct by construction, action metric neutral), or wait for the producer-aware AWQ fix.
3. DiT and Pi0 graphs: per-phase graphs with a host-owned loop are built (default taken); confirm the compilers team accepts a host loop, or asks for an unrolled whole-inference graph (not built). Also confirm what to do about the unreproducible recorded fixtures.
4. Matrix unit for projections (MXU0 versus MXU1), `nc` versus reference rule, bias handling, `lm_head`, BF16 opt-out list, `panel` naming.
5. Hardware and flow owners: the 12% to 45% device error against BF16 on long-K Pi0 layers (Gemma `down`, SigLIP `fc2`) that exact FP8 math does not show (accumulation order or precision on long K?), and the Gemma `down` layer 7 activation flush (83% of non-zero activations; a static-exponent or BF16 opt-out question). Also: rescale path (H1), exponent derivation on chip (H2), K-varying scales (H3), pop saturation hole, FP8 maximum 448 versus 240.
